# Motion References

The reference websites contain subtle motion and interaction that
cannot be fully represented by the screenshots.

The goal is NOT to reproduce their animations exactly.

Use them as inspiration for restrained, premium corporate motion.

## Desired motion language

Use subtle motion such as:

- slow image reveals as sections enter the viewport
- restrained fade/translate reveals for typography
- very subtle image scale or crop movement
- smooth navigation/header state transitions
- elegant button and text-link hover states
- subtle movement between image-led sections
- smooth but natural scrolling experience
- occasional mask/clip reveals where appropriate

Motion should feel deliberate and expensive rather than decorative.

## Avoid

Do not use:

- excessive fade-in animations on every element
- bounce effects
- exaggerated parallax
- scroll hijacking
- constant floating objects
- cursor gimmicks
- large spring animations
- excessive Framer Motion effects
- animations that delay access to content

Animations should support hierarchy and storytelling.

They should not call attention to themselves.
